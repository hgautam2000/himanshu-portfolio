Desi SNL

A sketch show for and by desis

“A sketch comedy show for and by desis.”

Created and directed by Azhar Bande-Ali, Desi SNL brings together writers, actors, and comedians from the South Asian diaspora to make people laugh at the things everyone recognizes but nobody's said out loud — original sketches performed live, through the lens of American late-night comedy. I'm a recurring cast member.

More From the Show

Beyond the stage

On September 25, 2026, Desi SNL released “Turkey,” an original comedy rap. I co-wrote it and I’m on lead vocals under my artist name, Himmy G, and it’s streaming on Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, and YouTube Music, with a full music video on YouTube. The show is produced by Azhar Bande-Ali (director/producer), Shreya Thakur, and Reuben Alexander, with a recurring ensemble including Shivani Shah and Reuben Alexander.
