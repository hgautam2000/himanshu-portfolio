Desi SNL

“A sketch comedy show for and by desis.”

Desi SNL is a live sketch show created and directed by Azhar Bande-Ali, with an all–South Asian company of writers, actors, and comedians. It borrows the shape of American late-night — cold open, monologue, Weekend Update — and fills it with family group chats, aunties, and wedding politics. I'm a recurring cast member.
