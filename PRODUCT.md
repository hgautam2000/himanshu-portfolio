# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Casting directors (primary).** Casting film, TV, theater, and commercial projects. They land from a submission, an email signature, or a social link, scan quickly, and decide whether to bring Himanshu in. They need headshots, credits, and on-camera footage in seconds, plus a way to reach him.
- **Talent agents and managers.** Evaluating whether to represent him. They look for range, credits, proof of momentum, and professionalism.
- **Comedy and show producers.** Booking sketch, live, or collaborative work. They look for comedic voice and live credits.
- **Fans and podcast listeners.** People who saw Desi SNL or heard Yappy Yaar and want more. Secondary audience; served, but never at the expense of the casting path.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Himanshu Gautam, a Brooklyn-based actor. Its job is to **get him auditions**. Success means a casting director can see his look, credits, and on-camera work, and contact him, without hunting.

## Positioning

**Actor first.** Comedy (Desi SNL), the Yappy Yaar podcast, and modeling are supporting evidence of range, presence, and comedic timing, not equal headline identities. He is a recurring cast member of Desi SNL, a sold-out live sketch comedy show for and by South Asian diaspora performers in New York City.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from casting submissions, emails, DMs, and social bios, often on a phone.
- Casting norms: headshot, resume/credits, reel, and contact are the expected core. Measurements and comp-card specs matter for modeling and commercial work.
- Union status is deliberately **not shown** on the site.
- Representation status is undecided (currently a placeholder "[Agency / Unrepped]").

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS site, no build step, six pages: Home, Theater, Short Films, Modeling, Podcast, About. Hostable as-is on any static host.
- Nav markup is duplicated in every page.
- Contact today is an email (with a copy button) and an Instagram handle; both are still placeholders.

## Product Principles

1. **Casting director first.** Every page answers "should we bring him in?" fast: face, credits, footage, contact.
2. **Actor leads; the rest is range.** Comedy, podcast, and modeling support the acting story rather than competing with it.
3. **Personality is the differentiator.** Playful copy is allowed and encouraged, as long as it never hides the facts.
4. **Only real proof.** Nothing placeholder, invented, or dead-linked is shown to visitors. Missing content is hidden until it exists.
5. **Phone-ready.** It must work on a phone, because links often get opened from DMs and emails.
